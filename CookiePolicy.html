Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 18th, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Yellow Dog Man Studios s.r.o. ("Company," "we," "us," and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our websites and services. It explains what these technologies are and why we use them, as well as your rights to control our use of them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Cookies are widely used by website owners in order to make their websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

Cookies set by us are known as "first-party cookies." Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies." Third-party cookies enable third-party features or functionality to be provided on or through the website (e.g. analytics). The parties that set these third-party cookies can recognize your computer when it visits our websites

Why do we use cookies?

We use first and third-party cookies for several reasons. Some cookies are required for technical reasons in order for our Website to operate, and we refer to these as "essential" or "strictly necessary" cookies. Other cookies also enable us to track and target the interests of our users to enhance the experience on our Online Properties. Third parties serve cookies through our website for analytics, and other purposes.

How can I control cookies?

On our websites, you can click the "Reject" / "Disallow" / "Block" buttons on cookie consent banners and popups. This will prevent us from using non-essential cookies.

You can also disable cookies in your browser's settings:

  • Chrome
  • Internet Explorer
  • Firefox
  • Safari
  • Edge
  • Opera

This will disable essential cookies. Which may require you to login or reject consent banners repeatedly.

What are essential cookies?

Essential cookies, are required to enable our websites to operate. Examples of Essential cookies are:

  • Authentication Cookies - These maintain your Login Session so you do not need to login each time you visit.
  • Consent Cookies - These record your cookie consent status and enable us to not ask you for consent each time you visit.
